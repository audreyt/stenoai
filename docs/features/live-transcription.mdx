---
title: "Live transcription"
description: "Apple SpeechTranscriber and Parakeet show live on-device text while you record. Switch Traditional Chinese live and ask questions before the meeting ends."
---

Live transcription shows the meeting as people speak. On macOS 26+, Apple SpeechTranscriber is the default and uses the system's speech assets. Parakeet provides the live fallback on older supported Macs and Windows.

## How it works

The renderer sends separate microphone and system-audio channels to the active live engine. Final segments retain timestamps and `[You]` or `[Others]` attribution. Changing the language during Apple transcription drains the current sidecar and restarts it with the selected locale, so a `zh-Hant` switch takes effect during the same recording.

The Ask bar remains active while recording. Each question uses finalized segments available at that moment; partial words stay out of the prompt. Answers stream from the configured LLM endpoint and can be cancelled.

## Engine requirements

Apple live transcription requires macOS 26+. Parakeet provides live transcription where Apple SpeechTranscriber is unavailable. Whisper remains post-stop only. See [Transcription models](/models/transcription-models) for details.

---

<Accordion title="Does the live transcript get saved?">
When you stop recording, Steno finalizes the recording into the saved transcript under `~/Library/Application Support/stenoai/transcripts/`, and your notes are generated from it. The live view is what you see during the meeting.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is any audio sent to a server for live transcription?">
No. Live transcription runs entirely on your Mac, the same as the rest of Steno's on-device pipeline.
</Accordion>
