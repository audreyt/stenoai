Steno transcribes on-device with three engines. **Apple SpeechTranscriber** is the default on macOS 26+ and uses system-managed locale assets. **Parakeet** remains the live fallback for older macOS versions and Windows. **Whisper** is the broad-language post-stop option. Choose the engine in **Settings → Transcribe**.

## How it works

Apple SpeechTranscriber and Parakeet produce a live transcript while you record. Steno finalizes the recording again after stop for the saved transcript. Whisper runs after recording. All engines process audio locally and return timestamped text.

## Language support

Language coverage depends on the selected engine.

- **Apple SpeechTranscriber (default on macOS 26+)** exposes 45 locales on the current runtime, including English, Traditional and Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, and several Indic languages. `Auto` follows the Mac system locale; concrete language pins select a specific model.
- **Parakeet** covers 25 European languages and remains the live engine on Windows.
- **Whisper** covers 99 languages and runs after stop.

## Speaker labels

Transcripts include `[You]` and `[Others]` labels when system audio is enabled. `[You]` labels lines from your microphone; `[Others]` labels the audio from your speakers.

## Viewing the transcript

The full transcript appears in the right pane of any saved note, below the AI summary. Transcripts are also saved as plain text files in `~/Library/Application Support/stenoai/transcripts/`.

<Accordion title="Does Steno transcribe in real time?">
Yes. Apple SpeechTranscriber and Parakeet produce live text. Whisper transcribes after you stop.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I edit the transcript?">
Transcripts are saved as plain text files. You can open and edit them in any text editor. Changes to the transcript file are reflected in Steno the next time you open the note.
</Accordion>
