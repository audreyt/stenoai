Steno processes audio locally through Apple SpeechTranscriber, Parakeet, or Whisper.

## The three engines

| Engine | Model storage | Notes |
|--------|---------------|-------|
| **Apple SpeechTranscriber** (default on macOS 26+) | System-managed locale assets | Live and batch transcription; 45 locales on the current runtime; no app model download |
| **Parakeet TDT v3** | ~572MB MLX on Apple Silicon; ~670MB ONNX on Windows | Live transcription; 25 European languages |
| **Whisper Large V3 Turbo** | ~1.6GB local weight | Post-stop transcription; 99 languages |

Apple SpeechTranscriber gives the fork its default path on current Macs. Parakeet and Whisper remain available in **Settings → Transcribe** for portability, unsupported locales, and older systems.

All three engines keep audio on the device.

## How to change engines

1. Open **Settings** in Steno
2. Select **Transcribe**
3. Choose **Apple On-Device**, **Parakeet TDT v3**, or **Whisper Large V3 Turbo**
4. Steno prepares the selected system asset or downloads the selected fallback model when required

The engine applies to future recordings. Changing the language during an active Apple recording restarts the live native lane with the new locale.

## Speed

Apple SpeechTranscriber and Parakeet both produce live text. Whisper transcribes after the recording ends.

## Language support

Language coverage depends on the engine you choose.

- **Apple SpeechTranscriber** supports 45 locales on the current macOS runtime, including Traditional Chinese (`zh_TW`), Simplified Chinese (`zh_CN`), Japanese, Korean, and multiple European and Indic locales.
- **Parakeet** covers 25 European languages and remains the live engine on Windows.
- **Whisper** covers 99 languages for post-stop transcription.

Apple `Auto` follows the system locale rather than detecting spoken language. Pin a concrete language when the meeting language differs from the Mac locale.

## Accuracy tips

- Choose a concrete Apple locale for a consistent meeting language.
- Capture system audio directly for virtual meetings instead of relying on speaker playback into the microphone.
- Use Whisper when the required language is outside Apple and Parakeet coverage.

---

<Accordion title="Which engine should I start with?">
Start with **Apple On-Device** on macOS 26+. Use Parakeet for its European-language live fallback or Windows, and Whisper for broader post-stop language coverage.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I switch engines for future recordings?">
Yes. Change the engine in **Settings → Transcribe** -- it applies to recordings from that point on. There is currently no way to re-transcribe an already-processed recording with a different engine; you can regenerate its summary, but not its transcript.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does transcription accuracy vary by language?">
Yes. Coverage and accuracy vary by locale and engine. Pin the Apple locale when you know the meeting language; switch to Parakeet or Whisper when that language is outside the available Apple set.
</Accordion>
